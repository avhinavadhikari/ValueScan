The knowledge base

Every great investor
left a playbook.
We run them all.

Benjamin Graham · The Intelligent Investor
"The margin of safety is the central concept of investment."
We apply this: every stock must trade meaningfully below our estimate of intrinsic value before it makes the list.
Warren Buffett · Berkshire Letters
"It's far better to buy a wonderful company at a fair price than a fair company at a wonderful price."
We apply this: our Buffett Score rates moat quality, management, predictability, and capital allocation — not just valuation.
Peter Lynch · One Up On Wall Street
"The person that turns over the most rocks wins the game."
We apply this: we screen small and mid-cap companies most institutions ignore — our Hidden Gems list lives here.
Charlie Munger · Poor Charlie's Almanack
"Invert, always invert. What would have to be true for this to be a bad investment?"
We apply this: every report includes a bear case — we argue the opposite before we make the case for value.
Seth Klarman · Margin of Safety
"Value investing is at its core the marriage of a contrarian streak and a calculator."
We apply this: we look hardest at hated sectors and beaten-down industries where fear has created opportunity.
Joel Greenblatt · The Little Book That Beats the Market
"Figure out the value of something and then pay a lot less."
We apply this: return on capital and earnings yield are the two primary quantitative lenses in our scoring engine.
Philip Fisher · Common Stocks and Uncommon Profits
"The best time to sell a stock is almost never."
We apply this: we screen for businesses with durable competitive advantages that can compound for a decade, not a quarter.
Mohnish Pabrai · The Dhandho Investor
"Heads I win, tails I don't lose much."
We apply this: our Buy Zone alert fires only when a stock enters a risk/reward position where downside appears genuinely limited.
